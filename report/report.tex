\section*{Introduction}
	
At the Faculty of Computer and Information Science (UL FRI), with approximately 2,000 enrolled students, the student office receives numerous recurring questions that could be efficiently addressed through an automated system. However, general-purpose conversational AI tools often lack the institutional context needed to answer faculty-specific questions accurately, leading to irrelevant responses.
In this project, we propose to develop a domain-specific conversational assistant designed to handle the most common inquiries from students at UL FRI. The system uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to make responses based on verified institutional sources, including the official FRI FAQ pages, study rules, and administrative guidelines. Our assistant aims to deliver accurate, context-aware answers while redirecting out-of-scope queries to human staff.

The primary motivation for this project is to reduce the burden on student office staff and provide students with instant, reliable answers at any time. the RAG approach allows us to work with a relatively small corpus of institutional documents while still having the generative capabilities of modern large language models.

\section*{Related Work}

In higher education, RAG-based chatbots have been successfully applied to student support. For instance, the College RAG Chatbot ingests college brochures, FAQs, and guides to answer admissions and academic queries accurately from verified sources \cite{college_rag_chatbot_github}.

To address complex queries over large document collections, Lee \cite{lee2025transforming} proposes a RAG framework that decomposes multihop questions into single-hop subquestions using an LLM.
Closer to home, the UL FS example chatbot demonstrates practical institutional use for student queries, inspiring our FRI-specific adaptation. These works validate RAG's effectiveness for administrative chatbots (\url{https://www.fs.uni-lj.si/en/}).
